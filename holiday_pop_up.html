Holiday Pop Up Market

Posted on December 1, 2024
  • Facebook
  • Twitter
  • Email
  • Calendar
  • Google Calendar
This event has already taken place.

Hello All, Santa & Mrs. Claus are taking a break ​from the Parade until it's time to deliver gifts, but the elves are busy in their workshop! In the spirit of the holiday season, please help the elves by donating ​new unwrapped toys, non-perishable food/pet items, or by making a monetary donation (see QR codes on the poster)

Please drop off at 513 Jones Ave, 44 Earl Grey Rd. 34 Hunter St. Starting November 25 - December 18, or at our Pocket Pop-Up Holiday Market Saturday, December 14, from 11-3pm. at Eastview Community Centre.
In support of Eastview Neighbourhood Community Centre & Toronto Humane Society.

Together we can continue to support our community especially during this holiday season. Special thanks to Donna Lee, Naomi Szeben, Ava Morgan, Eastview Community Centre.
