Jun 1
Sun, 1:00pm EDT

Eco Fun Fair + Community Pot-Luck Dinner

Pocket Change’s annual Eco FUN Fair is a great day to be outdoors and enjoy a wide variety of fun activities, all dedicated to protecting and celebrating our beautiful planet. It’s free and all are welcome!

Date: Sunday June 1 (rain date: June 8)
Time: Eco Fun Fair: 1-5pm; Community Potluck Dinner to start at 5:45pm. (more details below)

Place: Phin Park


Eco-Fun Fair:

  • There will be great speakers (including Walter H. Kehm, world-renown landscape architect who did the Master Plan for Tommy Thompson Park and many more iconic natural parklands, and a talk by a Pocket family who went “100% electric” with their home), a Home Energy Zone where you can learn about green home technologies and enter to win a free home energy audit, kids' activities including face-printing and performances from storyteller Sage Turtle, e-bike demos, bike swap & sale, bike safety checks, massive clothing swap, plant-based cookie tasting (while supplies last), and much more!
  • Check out all the details here: https://www.pocketchangeproject.ca/2025_ecofunfair And clothing swap details here: https://www.pocketchangeproject.ca/2025clothingswap 

Community Potluck Dinner: 

Please arrive @ 5:45pm with your dish; dinner will start around 6pm Bring your own reusable cutlery and plate. Water bottle refill station available and some beverages (juice, pop, sparkling water) available - pwyc. Bring a pot-luck dish to share and a serving spoon/implement for it if needed (label your container/implement so you can find them again to take home). Vegan/vegetarian contributions welcome! Suggestions for dish distribution:

  • Last name ending in A-G - bring a dessert!
  • Last name ending in H-0 - bring a main!
  • Last name ending in P-Z - bring an appetizer, salad, or side!

Questions, or want to volunteer at the Eco Fair? Email us at [email protected]
