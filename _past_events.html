Eco Fun Fair + Community Pot-Luck Dinner

Pocket Change’s annual Eco FUN Fair is a great day to be outdoors and enjoy a wide variety of fun activities, all dedicated to protecting and celebrating our beautiful planet. It’s free and all are welcome!

Date: Sunday June 1 (rain date: June 8)
Time: Eco Fun Fair: 1-5pm; Community Potluck Dinner to start at 5:45pm. (more details below)

Place: Phin Park


Eco-Fun Fair:

  • There will be great speakers (including Walter H. Kehm, world-renown landscape architect who did the Master Plan for Tommy Thompson Park and many more iconic natural parklands, and a talk by a Pocket family who went “100% electric” with their home), a Home Energy Zone where you can learn about green home technologies and enter to win a free home energy audit, kids' activities including face-printing and performances from storyteller Sage Turtle, e-bike demos, bike swap & sale, bike safety checks, massive clothing swap, plant-based cookie tasting (while supplies last), and much more!
  • Check out all the details here: https://www.pocketchangeproject.ca/2025_ecofunfair And clothing swap details here: https://www.pocketchangeproject.ca/2025clothingswap 

Community Potluck Dinner: 

Please arrive @ 5:45pm with your dish; dinner will start around 6pm Bring your own reusable cutlery and plate. Water bottle refill station available and some beverages (juice, pop, sparkling water) available - pwyc. Bring a pot-luck dish to share and a serving spoon/implement for it if needed (label your container/implement so you can find them again to take home). Vegan/vegetarian contributions welcome! Suggestions for dish distribution:

  • Last name ending in A-G - bring a dessert!
  • Last name ending in H-0 - bring a main!
  • Last name ending in P-Z - bring an appetizer, salad, or side!

Questions, or want to volunteer at the Eco Fair? Email us at [email protected]

Pocket Clean Up

Phin Park

Saturday April 26, 10 – 11:30 am: Pocket Green Space Clean-Up & 12-1 pm TRCA’s free "Gardening in a Changing Climate" Workshop

Bring your rakes, shovels and wheelbarrows to help us "tidy" (without disturbing wintering insects!) Phin Park, the Designated Off-Leash Area (dog park) and Oakvale Green, and to take home free compost (courtesy of Councillor Paula Fletcher). There'll be free beverages and baked goods (treats and monetary donations welcome). Work gloves recommended!

From 12 to 1 pm, join us for an engaging in-person workshop led by TRCA Pocket SNAP staff. You’ll discover ways to create climate-resilient landscapes at home. Learn about techniques like rainwater harvesting, native plant gardening, rain gardens, and permeable paving to help your garden thrive. Whether you’re a seasoned green thumb or just starting out, this hands-on session will empower you to cultivate a more sustainable, eco-friendly outdoor space! Please RSVP using this link: http://www.eventbrite.ca/e/gardening-in-a-changing-climate-workshop-tickets-1301260879319

Spring Fling 2025

Friday, April 18, 10 am – 12 pm – Clean-Up for Easter Egg Hunt

Come out for the Pocket's annual Spring Fling in Phin Park!

  • Note: Parents wishing to have their children participate in the Easter egg hunt must fill 20 plastic eggs (no *gold" ones) with a candy or toy for each child and must come at 9:00 am to hid your eggs.
  • 10:00 am Egg hunt (separate area for children <5) & photos with the Easter Bunny!!!

More information will be on our website soon: www.thepocket.ca/events 

We can’t do this without your help! We're calling Pocket artisans, volunteers, and students who need community hours to please reach out ASAP to confirm your interest [email protected]. Thanks go to sponsor Donna Lee and other volunteers who have signed up to help!
