We will be honouring the life of our friend, Jeff Otto (aka often referred to as the “mayor of the pocket”) in Phin Park. Details below. Rain date will be Saturday June 28... we will update the PCA Facebook and this page if we need to use the rain date. 

We will need volunteers to pull this event off (e.g. picking up chairs from Eastview, setting up in the park, etc). If you are interested, please email the PCA ([email protected]) or Dean Miller ([email protected]).
