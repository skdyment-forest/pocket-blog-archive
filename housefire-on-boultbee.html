House Fire on Boultbee

On Friday afternoon (February 21, 2014), there was a house fire on Boultbee Avenue.  The fire started in the attic at 62 Boultbee, and Toronto's fire service was able to stop it from spreading.

Executives from the PCA checked to see if they had a place to sleep or if they needed any other kind of assistance and asked them to call if we could help. The residents of both affected houses are safe and sound.
